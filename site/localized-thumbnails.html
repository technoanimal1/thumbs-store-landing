The problem

A translated site with English thumbnails is still half-translated.

Asian markets are huge, mobile-first and multilingual. You already translate the UI. But the lobby — the part players actually look at — is a wall of English game names.

1

The player switches language

A player in Bangkok, Seoul, Tokyo or Shanghai sets your lobby to their own language. Menus, buttons and help pages all follow.

2

The thumbnails don’t

Every game tile still shows its English logo. The one thing a player scans to pick a game is the one thing they can’t read.

3

They can’t find their game

So they scroll past, stick to the three titles they recognize, or bounce. Recognition drops, and so does play on everything else.

The fix

Thumbnails that speak the player’s language.

Because every thumbnail is rendered from a template, the title is text — not baked-in artwork. So we can set it in any script and redraw the logotype to match: same frame, same art, localized title.
