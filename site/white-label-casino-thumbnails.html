White label casino thumbnails, sold under your name.

With white label casino thumbnails, a platform, aggregator or agency sells lobby artwork to its own operators under its own name. thumbs.store supplies the catalogue, the brand templates, the production, the new releases and the delivery.

How the arrangement works

Your operators buy thumbnails from you. You decide the offer, the price and how it sits alongside your other services. We produce the artwork and keep it current.

For your operators it is one more service from a supplier they already work with. For you it is a product you can sell without hiring designers or building a rendering pipeline.

What thumbs.store supplies

The catalogue

10,000+ casino game thumbnails from 80+ providers, built from each game’s original key art and logotype.

Brand templates

A template per operator: frame, colours, corner radius and badge. Every game is rendered on it, in the sizes that operator uses.

Production

We render the thumbnails. Your team does not draw, crop or export anything.

New releases

New games are added as providers launch them, so your operators’ lobbies do not fall behind.

Delivery

An API and CDN feed, or file download, in PNG, WebP and AVIF.

Support for your business

We support your thumbnail business as the supplier behind it. You own the relationship with your operators.

Who it is for

  • Platform providers that run the casino back end for many brands and want lobby artwork to be part of the package.
  • Game aggregators that already deliver the games and want to deliver matching thumbnails with them.
  • Agencies and design studios that serve casino operators and need to cover thousands of games without drawing each one.

What your operators get

Each operator gets a lobby where every tile follows one design, whichever provider made the game. The template is theirs: two operators on your platform can look completely different while drawing on the same catalogue.

Delivery is the same as for our direct customers. Operators who integrate get images from the casino game thumbnails API; operators who prefer files get a download. You can also offer animated casino thumbnails as an add-on.

The template itself is edited in the studio, and the providers page shows which studios the catalogue covers. A white-label dashboard for platforms is on our roadmap; until it is generally available we agree the working setup with each partner.

How it is priced

For reference, our direct prices are public: the full catalogue is $1 per thumbnail, less at volume, and a custom brand design is a one-time $500. See the pricing page for the full list.

Questions buyers ask

Do our operators see the thumbs.store name?
No. You sell the thumbnails to your operators under your own name. thumbs.store works behind you as the supplier.
Who sets the price our operators pay?
You do. We quote you a partner price, and what you charge your operators is your decision.
Can each operator have its own look?
Yes. Each operator gets its own brand template, with its own frame, colours and badge, and every game is rendered on it.
How are new game releases handled?
New releases are added to the catalogue as providers launch them and rendered on each operator's template, so your operators receive them without filing a design request.
What does white label cost?
White label is priced per partner, on request. Tell us roughly how many operators and games you expect and we will send a quote.
